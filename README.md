Retail Analytics and Quick Commerce Optimization 
Project Overview
This project implements a comprehensive SQL-based analytics system for a retail quick commerce platform. It focuses on data-driven decision-making and business optimization across various aspects of the e-commerce operation.
Key Features
Database Design
Designed and implemented a robust relational database schema with 9 interconnected tables
Tables include: products, customers, orders, customer_feedback, order_items, delivery_performance, inventory, marketing_performance, and rating_icon
Advanced Analytics
Customer Insights
Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) calculation
RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis for customer segmentation
Cohort analysis for customer retention rates
Churn prediction based on order recency and feedback sentiment
Inventory Management
Seasonal demand trend analysis
Stock alert system for products needing immediate restock
Shelf life monitoring to identify products approaching expiration
Inventory turnover ratio calculation
Sales and Revenue Analysis
Revenue growth analysis with month-over-month comparisons
Cross-selling analysis to identify frequently bought together product pairs
Price optimization by comparing product pricing to category averages
Marketing Performance
Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) analysis for marketing campaigns
Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) calculation per marketing channel
Analysis of promotional effectiveness on sales velocity
Delivery and Logistics
Delivery partner performance scoring
Optimization of delivery zones based on distance and performance
Analysis of delivery times by hour of the day
Product Performance
Product return rate analysis
Financial loss calculation from damaged stock
Customer Feedback Analysis
Sentiment analysis of customer feedback
Identification of common feedback patterns
Analysis of negative feedback trends
Technologies Used
SQL (PostgreSQL syntax)
Advanced SQL features: CTEs, Window Functions, Subqueries
Potential Applications
This project demonstrates proficiency in SQL, data modeling, and business analytics, showcasing the ability to translate complex business requirements into actionable data insights for quick commerce optimization.
